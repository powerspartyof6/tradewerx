Fix it guide

Why is my refrigerator not cooling but the freezer still works?

A warm fresh food side with a cold freezer points at airflow between compartments, not the compressor. The usual causes are a failed evaporator fan, a defrost fault icing the coil, or a damper stuck closed. If the freezer holds, the sealed system is almost always fine and the repair is a component replacement.

Check these first

Four things to try before you book

None of these require tools or take more than a few minutes. If one of them fixes it, you have saved a service call.

  1. Clear the interior vents of food so air can move between compartments.
  2. Vacuum the condenser coils behind the toe kick or at the top of a built in unit.
  3. Listen for the evaporator fan running inside the freezer. Silence usually means a failed fan.
  4. Check that the doors close fully and the gaskets seal along the whole edge.

Still not working

When to call

If the fault persists or returns within a day, a component has failed and needs to be tested and replaced. Send the model and serial number from the data plate, the exact symptom and any code on the display.
