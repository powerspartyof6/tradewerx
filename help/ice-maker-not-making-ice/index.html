Fix it guide

Why is my ice maker not making ice?

Scale on the evaporator is the most common cause of thin, hollow or slow ice in under counter machines. If water is reaching the unit and the harvest cycle never completes, the thermostat or harvest assembly needs service.

Check these first

Four things to try before you book

None of these require tools or take more than a few minutes. If one of them fixes it, you have saved a service call.

  1. Confirm the water supply valve behind the unit is fully open and the line is not kinked.
  2. Replace a water filter that is past its service life.
  3. Run a cleaning cycle with the descaler the manufacturer specifies.
  4. Check that the bin sensor or arm is not blocked by a stack of cubes.
