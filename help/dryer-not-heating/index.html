Fix it guide

Why does my dryer take two cycles to dry a load?

Restricted venting causes this far more often than a failed heating element. Cleaning the duct restores airflow, cuts drying time and removes a fire risk. If the vent is clear and the drum still runs cool, the element, thermostat or thermal fuse is next.

Check these first

Four things to try before you book

None of these require tools or take more than a few minutes. If one of them fixes it, you have saved a service call.

  1. Clean the lint screen and the housing below it.
  2. Disconnect the vent at the back of the dryer and check for lint packed in the duct.
  3. Confirm the exterior vent hood opens freely when the dryer runs.
  4. On electric dryers, check that both breakers in the double pole are on.
