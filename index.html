Finding the data plate

Refrigerator: inside the fresh-food side, left wall or ceiling.

Dishwasher: on the tub edge, visible with the door open.

Range or oven: door jamb, or the frame behind the bottom drawer.

Washer or dryer: inside the door opening, or the rear panel.

Before we come out

Leave the appliance as it is. Undoing the fault makes it harder to confirm.

Clear a working path and empty the unit if it is a refrigerator or a washer.

An adult needs to be on site for the full visit.

Gas or water emergency

Smell gas or have water actively flooding? Shut off the supply and call your utility or 911 first. Do not wait on this form.
