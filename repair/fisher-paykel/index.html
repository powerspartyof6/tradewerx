  • Range igniter and oven heating faults

Before you call

Fix it guides for Fisher & Paykel owners

Dishwasher not draining

Remove and rinse the filter in the bottom of the tub.

Dryer not heating or taking two cycles

Clean the lint screen and the housing below it.

Washer not draining or spinning

Clear the drain pump filter at the bottom front of a front loader.

Dishwasher showing an error code

Note the exact code before turning the unit off.

Questions about Fisher & Paykel repair

Do you repair Fisher & Paykel appliances in North Carolina?

Yes. What Fisher & Paykel problems do you see most?

The most common Fisher & Paykel calls are DishDrawer F1 flood and drain faults, drawer motor and lid actuator service, CoolDrawer and column refrigeration temperature drift, and washer out of balance and drain errors. Each visit starts with error code retrieval and component testing rather than guessing at parts.

How do I book Fisher & Paykel service with Tradewerx?

Submit the request form with the model and serial number from the data plate, a description of the symptom and any code on the display. Photos of the plate can be read automatically. Tradewerx confirms the date by email.

Can Tradewerx handle a Fisher & Paykel repair that is covered by the manufacturer?

Ask Fisher & Paykel or your service plan administrator to send Tradewerx. Out of program work is booked directly through the request form.
