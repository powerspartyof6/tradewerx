Before you call

Fix it guides for Aga owners

Oven not heating or holding temperature

On gas, watch the igniter through the oven floor vent. A glow that never lights the burner means a weak igniter.

Questions about Aga repair

Do you repair Aga appliances in North Carolina?

Yes. Tradewerx services Aga cast iron heat storage cookers and dual fuel ranges throughout western North Carolina, including Lenoir, Hickory, Morganton, Boone, Asheville, Statesville and Charlotte, and travels into eastern Tennessee, upstate South Carolina and southern Virginia.

What Aga problems do you see most?

The most common Aga calls are element and control faults on electric models, oven temperature drift, door seal and hinge service, and hotplate and lid alignment. Each visit starts with error code retrieval and component testing rather than guessing at parts.

How do I book Aga service with Tradewerx?

Submit the request form with the model and serial number from the data plate, a description of the symptom and any code on the display. Photos of the plate can be read automatically. Tradewerx confirms the date by email.

Can Tradewerx handle a Aga repair that is covered by the manufacturer?

Ask Aga or your service plan administrator to send Tradewerx. Out of program work is booked directly through the request form.
